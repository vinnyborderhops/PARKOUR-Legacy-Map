How to get around

The camera works like Roblox Studio's: you fly it around, and it turns in place.

Right-dragLook around
W A S DFly forward, left, back, right (speeds up while held)
Q EDown, up
ShiftHold to move slowly
ScrollMove toward or away from where the mouse points
Left-dragPan (middle-drag works too)
Alt + Left-dragOrbit around the spot under the mouse
FFly to whatever is selected

On a touchscreen: one finger pans, two fingers pinch to zoom and drag to look.

Pick a trial, spawn, tower or secret from the list (or click its label on the map) to fly there and see its details. Props and map decor load as you get close; turn them on under Layers.
